Tableau Coffee Bean Analysis

In this Tableau project, I conducted a comprehensive analysis of coffee bean sales data across different regions and products. The analysis revealed key insights including total sales of $629,648 across 730 customers with an average sale of $862.5. The visualization breaks down market share by coffee origin, with Costa Rican coffee leading at 22.60%, followed by Colombian (20.82%), Brazilian (20.00%), Guatemalan (19.04%), and Ethiopian (17.53%) varieties. Monthly sales trends and customer distribution patterns were analyzed, with detailed metrics showing Colombian beans having the highest customer base (8,426), followed by Costa Rican (8,115), Brazilian (7,630), Guatemalan (6,966), and Ethiopian (6,582) varieties. This analysis provides valuable insights for inventory management, market targeting, and sales strategy optimization.

Power BI Coffee Bean Analysis

In this Power BI project, I developed an interactive dashboard analyzing coffee bean sales performance and market distribution. The analysis showcases total sales of 630K across 730 customers, with a total quantity sold of 19K units. The visualization includes detailed breakdowns of average unit prices by coffee origin, with Ethiopian beans commanding the highest price point at 45.00, followed by Colombian (40.00), while Costa Rica, Guatemala, and Brazilian varieties all averaged 35.00 per unit. The market share distribution is visualized through an intuitive donut chart, revealing balanced market segments across regions. Monthly sales trends are tracked through a dual-color bar chart, enabling easy comparison of performance across different periods. Additionally, the dashboard features an interactive map visualization showing geographical distribution of sales and market presence across the Middle East region.

Chocolate Sales EDA Python

In this Python-based Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project, I analyzed chocolate sales data to uncover trends, patterns, and insights in the chocolate industry. Leveraging a comprehensive set of Python libraries including Pandas, Matplotlib, Plotly Express, Seaborn, NumPy, Altair, and Scikit-learn, I created interactive visualizations and performed detailed statistical analysis. The project utilized advanced data visualization techniques through libraries like Plotly and Chart Studio for interactive plots, while Scikit-learn enabled sophisticated data analysis. Additional libraries such as datetime were used for temporal analysis, making this a robust exploration of chocolate sales patterns and market dynamics.
